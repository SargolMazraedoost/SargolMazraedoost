## About Me

I am a **Full-Time Research Professor (전임연구교수)** based in South Korea, holding a **Ph.D. in Chemical Convergence Engineering**.

My research focuses on **artificial intelligence (AI)-driven cheminformatics and bioinformatics**, integrating machine learning, deep learning, quantitative structure–property relationships (QSPR), quantitative structure–activity relationships (QSAR), quantitative structure–retention relationships (QSRR), molecular docking, and molecular dynamics simulations.

My work encompasses **molecular property prediction, chromatographic retention time modeling, and protein–ligand and protein–protein interactions**, with an emphasis on developing predictive, interpretable, and computationally efficient approaches to molecular sciences.

My broader research objective is to bridge **artificial intelligence, computational chemistry, and molecular modeling** to advance our understanding of complex chemical and biological systems.

## Selected Research Projects

A selection of my published research integrating artificial intelligence, computational chemistry, and molecular modeling to address challenges in chromatographic retention time prediction and molecular interactions.

<table>
<tr>

<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 01 · [RT-TR](https://github.com/SargolMazraedoost/RT-TR)

**SMILES → Retention Time**

A hybrid Transformer–Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) framework for predicting chromatographic retention times directly from molecular SMILES representations.

**Research Highlights**

- Dataset: **77,980 molecules**
- Hybrid Transformer–BiLSTM architecture
- Direct SMILES-based retention time prediction
- Application of deep learning to cheminformatics

**Methods**

`Transformer` `BiLSTM` `Deep Learning` `SMILES`

**Resources**

[Publication](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c00167) · [Source Code](https://github.com/SargolMazraedoost/RT-TR)

</td>

<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 02 · [Cross-Column QSRR](https://github.com/SargolMazraedoost/Cross-Column-QSRR-Machine-Learning)

**Molecular Descriptors → Retention Prediction**

A machine learning framework combining density functional theory-derived molecular descriptors and chromatographic parameters to predict retention behavior across reversed-phase HPLC columns.

**Research Highlights**

- Cross-column chromatographic modeling
- Quantum chemical molecular descriptors
- Predictive Q²: **0.989**
- RMSEP: **0.749 min**

**Methods**

`DFT` `QSRR` `Gradient Boosting` `RP-HPLC`

**Resources**

[Publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00216-024-05243-7) · [Repository](https://github.com/SargolMazraedoost/Cross-Column-QSRR-Machine-Learning)

</td>

<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 03 · [MBD2–p66α](https://doi.org/10.1002/bkcs.12923)

**Protein Mutations → Binding Affinity**

An integrative computational pipeline for identifying mutations predicted to enhance protein–protein binding affinity in the MBD2–p66α coiled-coil complex.

**Research Highlights**

- **565** mutations screened
- **10** candidates evaluated
- Stabilizing candidates: **K149I, K149L, K163L**
- Binding free-energy analysis

**Methods**

`Molecular Dynamics` `Alchemical Free Energy` `MM/PBSA` `Protein Engineering`

**Resources**

[Publication](https://doi.org/10.1002/bkcs.12923) · [Repository](https://github.com/SargolMazraedoost/MBD2-p66alpha-Binding-Affinity)

## Current Research Directions

My current computational research extends across several interconnected areas of molecular sciences.

### Molecular Representation Learning

Exploring molecular fingerprints, graph neural networks, molecular language models, and structure-aware representations for chemical property prediction.

### Molecular Docking & Molecular Dynamics

Investigating protein–ligand and protein–protein interactions using molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, and binding free-energy calculations.

### Quantum Chemistry & Molecular Properties

Integrating quantum chemical descriptors, physicochemical information, and machine learning to investigate and predict molecular properties.

### Chromatographic Retention Modeling

Developing transferable quantitative structure–retention relationship models and deep learning frameworks for chromatographic retention time prediction.

### Molecular Solvation & Free Energy

Investigating molecular solvation, solvent effects, intermolecular interactions, and thermodynamic properties through molecular dynamics and free-energy calculations.
