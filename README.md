## About Me

I am a **Full-Time Research Professor (전임연구교수)** in South Korea with a **Ph.D. in Chemical Convergence Engineering** from Pukyong National University.

My research focuses on **AI-driven cheminformatics and bioinformatics**, integrating **density functional theory (DFT), machine learning, and deep learning** for QSRR and QSAR modeling, together with **molecular docking and molecular dynamics simulations** for investigating protein–protein and protein–ligand interactions.

My broader goal is to combine **molecular information, physical chemistry, and artificial intelligence** to build predictive and interpretable computational models for chemical and biological systems.

## Current Research Directions

My current computational work extends across several areas of molecular AI and modeling:

**Molecular Representation Learning**
Exploring molecular fingerprints, graph neural networks, molecular language models, and 3D structure-aware representations for chemical property prediction.

**Molecular Docking & Dynamics**
Using docking, molecular dynamics simulations, and energetic analyses to investigate ligand–protein and protein–protein interactions.

**Quantum Chemistry & Molecular Properties**
Integrating DFT-derived molecular descriptors and physicochemical information with statistical and machine-learning models.

**Chromatographic Modeling**
Developing transferable QSRR and deep-learning approaches for predicting chromatographic retention behavior directly from molecular information.
